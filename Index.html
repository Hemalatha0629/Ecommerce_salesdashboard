Key findings

What the data revealed

Four concrete takeaways with direct strategy implications.

2×
Q4 revenue spike (Oct–Nov)
October and November consistently double the monthly average — driven by holiday gift purchasing. Supply chain and inventory planning should anchor to this window.
83%
Revenue concentrated in the UK
Despite 10 active markets, the UK accounts for £14.7M of £17.7M total. Germany and Netherlands represent the clearest near-term international growth targets with strong order counts.
72%
High customer retention rate
4,255 of 5,878 customers returned — a healthy signal for product-market fit. RFM segmentation identified a "champion" cluster worth prioritising for loyalty programmes.
£528K
Jan 2012 forecast (regression model)
Time-series model projects £528K for Jan 2012 — consistent with Jan 2010 and 2011 post-holiday dips. Forecast horizon extends through Q1 2012 with seasonal adjustment.
